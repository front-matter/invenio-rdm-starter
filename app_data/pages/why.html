• Running in 15 minutes — a prebuilt Docker image and a Docker Compose file with sensible defaults, no local Python or invenio-cli needed.
  • Configured, not coded — every setting is an environment variable, documented at starter.front-matter.de.
  • Persistent identifiers built in — people, organizations, journals, funders and grants come from Crossref, DataCite, ROR and ORCID metadata, with a page of their own.
  • Metrics — works, citations, licenses and more, for the whole repository and for every person, organization and journal.
  • Log in your way — ORCID, GitHub, Google or your own Keycloak or Authentik.
  • Open source — MIT licensed, and coordinated by the InvenioRDM partner Front Matter.
