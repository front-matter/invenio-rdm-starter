InvenioRDM Starter runs InvenioRDM, the open source turn-key research data management repository, from a prebuilt Docker image and a Docker Compose file with sensible defaults. It runs on a laptop within 15 minutes, and in production with the same configuration.

The Starter adds profiles of people, organizations and journals, metrics, and relations to funders and grants, from the persistent identifier metadata of Crossref, DataCite, ROR and ORCID.

InvenioRDM Starter is open source software under the MIT license, coordinated by the InvenioRDM partner Front Matter. The documentation describes every setting, and the source is on GitHub.
